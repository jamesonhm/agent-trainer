Yes—here’s a full-body, mostly bodyweight session, with bands as optional tools. You trained legs and shoulders yesterday (including 3 sets of Bulgarian split squats and pike pushups), so keep today easy: stop with about 3 reps in reserve, and avoid anything that causes shoulder pain or instability.

1. Squat (Bodyweight) — 2 × 10–12 @ bodyweight (~195 lb; no added load)
2. Bent Over Row (Band) — 2 × 12–15 @ light band tension. New for your log; if you don’t have a band, use Inverted Row only with a genuinely secure setup.
3. Chest Press (Band) — 2 × 10–12 @ light band tension, in a comfortable range. New for your log; without a band, substitute Incline Push Ups — 2 × 6–10 on a wall or sturdy counter, only if your shoulder feels stable.
4. Glute Bridge — 2 × 12–15 @ bodyweight (no added load)
5. Standing Calf Raise — 2 × 15–20 @ bodyweight (no added load)
6. Dead Bug — 2 × 8 per side @ bodyweight
Alternate 1–2, 3–4, then 5–6; rest 45–60 seconds after each pair. Bands don’t have a reliable lb target here, so use light tension and adjust by feel. Never improvise a row using unstable furniture. Offer to swap exercises or shorten it if needed.
